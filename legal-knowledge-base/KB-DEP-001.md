### Plain-Language Summary
Residential security deposit is capped at a 2-month rent benchmark under Section 11 of the Model Tenancy Act (adopted by urban civil courts). Under Karnataka Rent Act Sections 15 and 16, landlords cannot retain unreasonable advances. Excess holding beyond 2 months is refundable. Deductions are permissible solely for agreement-authorized damage with itemised GST repair invoices, not ordinary wear and tear.

### Statutory Provision
Model Tenancy Act, 2021 Section 11 (2-month residential deposit cap benchmark); Karnataka Rent Act, 1999 Sections 15 & 16 (advance holding & statutory receipts); Indian Contract Act, 1872 Section 73 (refund of withheld amounts).
