### Plain-Language Summary
Security deposit must be reasonable. Excess beyond the customary 2-month residential benchmark is refundable. Landlord may deduct only for documented, itemised, agreement-authorised damage — not ordinary wear/tear, aging paint, or routine maintenance.

### Statutory Provision
Karnataka Rent Act, 1999 Sections 15 & 16 (binding in Karnataka). Reference comparison only: Model Tenancy Act Section 11 (not controlling in Karnataka).

**Use:** Module A — Security deposit recovery notices. Never declare painting deductions automatically illegal; assess wear/tear vs contractual liability.
