### Plain-Language Summary
Rent revisions and demands must be served through formal written instrument (registered post / email with acknowledgment / legal notice). WhatsApp/email can supplement but are insufficient alone. Confirm official Rent Controller jurisdiction from DPAL / Revenue Department notification per PIN — do not assume every PIN maps to the same forum.

### Statutory Provision
Karnataka Rent Act applicable notice rules; Indian Contract Act general notice / agreement enforcement.
