### Plain-Language Summary
Landlord must provide itemised GST-compliant repair invoices for any deduction. Normal wear, tear, aging paint, or routine maintenance cannot be deducted arbitrarily. Unverified withholding = breach of contract; tenant entitled to refund + interest.

### Statutory Provision
Karnataka Rent Act Sections 15/16 (binding). Indian Contract Act Sections 37 & 73 (national contract breach / compensation framework).
