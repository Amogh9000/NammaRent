# Karnataka Rent Act, 1999 — Key Sections

Binding in Karnataka only. Not a national statute.

- Section 7 & 9: Rent revision / agreement-based increases.
- Section 15 & 16: Security deposit rules (binding for Karnataka residential tenancies).
- Section 27: Eviction / utility disconnection protection.
- Section 49: Essential-service restoration.
- Section 54: Penalties for violations.

Always use this over Model Tenancy Act (Section 11) when jurisdiction is Karnataka.
