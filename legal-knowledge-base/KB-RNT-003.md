### Plain-Language Summary
Rent increase must comply with agreement clause and Karnataka Rent Act Section 7/Section 9. If agreement fixed-term applies, landlord cannot unilaterally alter. Section 106 concerns termination of certain leases — not a general 90-day rent-increase notice rule.

### Statutory Provision
Karnataka Rent Act Sections 7 & 9 (binding). Transfer of Property Act Section 106 (termination / notice rules where applicable).
