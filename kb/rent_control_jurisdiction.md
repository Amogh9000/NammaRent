### Plain-Language Summary
In Bengaluru urban jurisdiction, disputes under the Karnataka Rent Act relating to eviction, recovery of possession, or illegal withholding of amenities fall under the jurisdiction of the Court of Small Causes (for premises over specified threshold areas/exemptions) and the designated Rent Controller (Assistant Commissioner / Sub-Divisional Magistrate). For emergency utility restoration or unauthorized lock-outs, the Rent Controller holds summary inquiry powers to pass interlocutory injunctive orders.
