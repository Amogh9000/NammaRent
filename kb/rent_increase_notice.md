### Plain-Language Summary
Any revision or enhancement of agreed rent must be preceded by at least ninety (90) days of prior written notice before the revised rent becomes effective. A landlord cannot unilaterally hike rent overnight, during an unexpired fixed-term lease period, or without delivering clear written notice specifying the quantum and lawful justification for the hike. If the notice is received with less than 90 days gap, the proposed revision cannot be enforced until the expiration of the full statutory notice period.
