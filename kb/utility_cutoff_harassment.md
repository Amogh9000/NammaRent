### Plain-Language Summary
A landlord is strictly prohibited from cutting off, withholding, or tampering with essential services and amenities enjoyed by the tenant in respect of the premises, including water supply (BWSSB), electricity (BESCOM), passage, elevator, or sanitary facilities, either by themselves or through an agent. Disconnecting utilities to force eviction or compel capitulation in a dispute is a cognizable legal violation. Tenants can immediately petition the Rent Controller for an emergency restoration order and lodge an emergency complaint with Karnataka Police (Dial 112).
