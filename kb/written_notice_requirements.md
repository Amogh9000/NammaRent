### Plain-Language Summary
Every notice concerning termination, deposit forfeiture, tenancy alteration, or demand for unpaid sums must be in writing, signed by or on behalf of the person giving it, and either tendered personally or sent through registered post with acknowledgement due, or documented via verifiable written electronic records (registered email/WhatsApp with delivery verification). Informal verbal claims or arbitrary WhatsApp threats lack evidentiary standing without explicit documentation and acknowledgment.
