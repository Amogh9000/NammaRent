### Plain-Language Summary
In residential tenancies, the security deposit paid by the tenant shall not exceed two months' rent as per national model tenancy benchmarks adopted across urban jurisdictions. The landlord is legally obligated to refund the security deposit to the tenant on the date of vacating and handing over vacant possession of the premises, after making agreed and legally permissible deductions. Arbitrary withholding of deposits without clear, itemised justification constitutes unjust enrichment.
