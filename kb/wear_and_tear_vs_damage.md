### Plain-Language Summary
A tenant is not liable for reasonable wear and tear arising from ordinary use, weathering, and passage of time (such as minor wall scuffs, natural paint fading, aging fittings). Landlords cannot unilaterally deduct arbitrary lump sums (e.g., standard one-month painting charges or deep cleaning fee) unless explicitly stipulated and justified by genuine physical damages caused by tenant negligence. For any legitimate deduction claimed, the landlord must furnish itemised, GST-compliant repair invoices or actual receipts.
