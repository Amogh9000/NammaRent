## Official Sources for Verification

1. **Karnataka Rent Act, 1999 (Karnataka Act No. 34 of 2001)**
   - Official Source: Karnataka State Gazette / Department of Parliamentary Affairs & Legislation
   - URL: `https://dpal.karnataka.gov.in/` / `http://dpal.kar.nic.in/`
   - Key Sections:
     - Section 27: Protection of essential services (electricity, water, sanitation).
     - Section 30 & Section 31: Jurisdiction of the Rent Controller / Rent Court.
     - Section 8: Written agreement and receipt of rent and deposit.

2. **Model Tenancy Act, 2021 (Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs - MoHUA, Govt. of India)**
   - Adopted Principles & Karnataka Cabinet Guidelines
   - Official Source: MoHUA Portal
   - URL: `https://mohua.gov.in/`
   - Key Sections:
     - Section 11: Security deposit cap (maximum 2 months rent for residential premises).
     - Section 12: Notice of revision of rent (at least 90 days in advance).
     - Section 15: Structural repairs vs day-to-day wear & tear; provision of itemised invoices.

3. **The Registration Act, 1908 & Transfer of Property Act, 1882**
   - Official Source: India Code (Legislative Department, Ministry of Law and Justice)
   - URL: `https://www.indiacode.nic.in/`
   - Key Sections:
     - Section 17 & 49 of Registration Act: Mandatory registration for leases exceeding 11 months.
     - Section 106 & 108 of Transfer of Property Act: Duration of certain leases and rights/liabilities of lessor and lessee.

4. **Bruhat Bengaluru Mahanagara Palike (BBMP) Building & Tenancy Bylaws**
   - Official Source: BBMP Administrative Portal
   - URL: `https://bbmp.karnataka.gov.in/`
