### Plain-Language Summary
Under the Registration Act 1908, any lease of immovable property from year to year, or for any term exceeding eleven (11) months, is compulsorily registrable at the Sub-Registrar's Office with requisite stamp duty paid. While 11-month agreements are commonly executed on non-judicial stamp paper or e-stamp certificates, failure to register longer-term leases limits their admissibility as direct evidence of lease terms in court, though collateral tenancy and possession rights remain protected under equity and civil law.
